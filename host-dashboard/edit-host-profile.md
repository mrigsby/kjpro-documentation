# Edit Host Profile

To edit your host profile details click on  \`Edit Profile\` in the dashboard navigation&#x20;

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-09-16 at 12.12.14 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### Profile Edit Form

The profile edit form contains four main sections

* Profile/Contact
* Address
* About You
* Your Business

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-09-16 at 12.13.20 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### Profile / Contact

This contains all your basic information as well as provides an area for you to provide links to all your socials that will be displayed on public side of the site.&#x20;

The Profile Photo allows you to upload a profile image by clicking the "Upload Profile Photo" button. This intended to be a picture of yourself. If you have a business logo that can be added in the "Your Business" tab. It will give you a preview and will be saved to the server when the form is saved.&#x20;

#### Address

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-09-16 at 12.16.21 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

This is your provided address

> NOTE: Your address is NEVER made public and only used by KJPro.info when appropiate. You can read out [Privacy Policy](https://kjpro.info/privacy-policy) for an elaboration on our policies.

#### About You

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-09-16 at 12.19.18 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The about you section is used for your Host Profile Page. In order to provide a more-than simple text option to make your profile much more polished the About You supports using Markdown. [For a simple guide on using Markdown it would be well worth your time to visit this link and get to know the basic use of Markdown. ](https://www.markdownguide.org/basic-syntax/)

#### Your Business

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-09-16 at 12.23.17 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The Your Business section provides the ability to provide your KJ/DJ Name (i.e. DJ Billy), a separate business name, busienss phone, mobile phone and a short business description.

A business logo can be uploaded using the "Select New Photo" button. It will give you a preview and will be saved to the server when the form is saved.&#x20;

The short business description should be kept to just a couple of sentences. This is intended to be more of a tag line type snippet. It is much better to go into details about services provided, experience, history, etc. in your About You section.&#x20;

#### Saving Your Profile

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-09-16 at 12.33.58 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Before saving your profile (as well as events, event modifiers, etc.) you will need to confirm that you agree with the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Once your profile is saved you will be shown a confirmation at the top of the page confirming the profile was saved.&#x20;
