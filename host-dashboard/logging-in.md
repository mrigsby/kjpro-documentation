# Logging In

Lets go over the basics... Logging in! You can click the "Host Login" in the website main menu or head over to [https://www.kjpro.info/account/login](https://www.kjpro.info/account/login)&#x20;

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-09-16 at 2.10.14 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Enter your email address and password and click "Login" and it will take you directly to your Host Dashboard

### Lost Password ?

If you cant remember your password click the "Lost Your Password? Click Here" link and you will be presented with the lost password form.&#x20;

<div data-full-width="false"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-09-16 at 2.10.28 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

Enter your email address and click "Request Password Reset" and the KJPro system will send you an email with a secure link to reset your password.&#x20;

Once you recieve the password reset email, follow the directions in the email, click on the link and enter your new password and confirm your new password in the second field. you can then click the button to change your password. You will be given a confirmation message and taken back to the login screen.

{% hint style="info" %}
Password reset links are only good for a single use and expire after 48 hours
{% endhint %}
